How the AI works

No black box. Every problem type, urgency score and ranking can be traced back to these six steps, all running in your browser.

  1. 1

    Clean the words

    Lower-case the text, drop filler words and trim endings, so leaking, leaked and leaks all count as leak. Word pairs like main street are kept too.

  2. 2

    Guess the problem type

    A Naive Bayes classifier compares the words with labelled example reports and picks the most likely type, with a confidence score.

  3. 3

    Score the urgency

    Risk signals like live wires, injuries, sewage or children nearby add points on a 0 to 10 scale. Every point is shown to the person reporting.

  4. 4

    Look for duplicates

    TF-IDF turns each report into weighted words. Cosine similarity, plus the distance between map pins, finds open reports about the same problem.

  5. 5

    Rank the work

    FixFirst score = urgency × 7 (up to 70) + residents affected (up to 20) + days waiting (up to 10). Critical hazards always go first.

  6. 6

    Learn from corrections

    When an official fixes a wrong problem type, that report joins the training data and the model retrains on the spot.
